How to play Whot

Match the shape or number of the top card.
1 · Hold On — play again.
2 · Pick Two — next player picks 2 (or answers with a 2).
5 · Pick Three — next player picks 3 (or answers with a 5).
8 · Suspension — next player misses a turn.
14 · General Market — everyone else picks 1.
20 · WHOT — wild card, call any shape.
Market — can't play? Tap MARKET to pick a card.
Win — empty your hand first. Others score points for cards left (stars count double).
